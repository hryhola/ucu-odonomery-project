# Sources

## Core

- **Hartley (1997), [In defense of the eight-point algorithm](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.601246)** — eight-point estimation and normalisation.
- **Hartley & Zisserman (2004), [Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision, 2nd ed.](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/hzbook/)** — epipolar geometry, pose recovery, RANSAC, and triangulation. Read §§4.4, 4.7, 9.6, 11.1–11.2, 11.9, 12.2, and A5.3.
- **Delmerico et al. (2019), [The UZH-FPV Drone Racing Dataset](https://rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/docs/ICRA19_Delmerico.pdf)** — dataset, sensors, and flight conditions.

## Supplementary

- **Demmel (1997), Applied Numerical Linear Algebra** — SVD and numerical stability; §§3.2.3, 3.3, and 3.5.
- **Nistér (2004), [An Efficient Solution to the Five-Point Relative Pose Problem](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2004.17)** — five-point baseline.
- [OpenCV documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html) — estimation, pose recovery, and camera models.
- [UZH-FPV data and calibration](https://fpv.ifi.uzh.ch/datasets/) — sequence selection, calibration, and ground truth.

PDFs are stored locally and excluded from Git.
